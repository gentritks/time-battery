School device guidelines

Laptop Usage Rules

Sign out of Kiosk Mode Ctrl + Alt + Delete

Please read and follow these rules:

  • Use this laptop only for assigned school activities.
  • No gaming, personal browsing, or social media.
  • Do not write or make notes on the laptop hardware.
  • Do not change settings or backgrounds.
  • Do not restart or turn off the computer unless instructed.
  • Do not eat or drink near the laptop.
  • Do not attach stickers, papers, or other objects to the laptop.
  • Do not remove school-issued name labels. Report damaged labels to IT.
  • Do not share your login details with others.
  • Sign out when you finish your work, unless instructed otherwise.
  • Tell a teacher or an IT staff member if something is not working.
  • Do not try to fix technical issues yourself.
  • Do not plug in USB drives or external devices without permission.
  • Always follow your teacher's or supervisor's directions.
  • Carry the laptop with two hands and close the lid gently.
  • Keep vents clear and use the laptop on a stable surface.
  • Use only the charger assigned to the laptop or approved by the school.
  • Report damage, loss, or unusual activity immediately.

Remember: All activity on this computer is monitored. Using it responsibly helps everyone!
